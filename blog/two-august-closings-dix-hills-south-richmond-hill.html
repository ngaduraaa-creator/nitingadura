Two August Closings: $950K in South Richmond Hill, $1.5M in Dix Hills — and What They Say About This Market

Closed-transaction report · Queens & Long Island. By Nitin Gadura, NYS Salesperson #10401383405. Published October 24, 2026.

The short version: in August 2026 I helped clients reach the closing table on two very different homes — a $950,000 single-family at 107-73 129th Street in South Richmond Hill (deed dated August 5, now on public record), and a $1,500,000 five-bedroom in Dix Hills, Long Island (closed August 18). Two price points, two markets, one month — and both closed while 30-year rates sat near 7%. Well-prepared deals are still getting done.

What two very different closings say about one market

Put side by side, these two deals from the same month make three points that matter if you're deciding whether to sell:

1. Deals are closing at real numbers despite 7% rates. Neither of these was a distress sale. Buyers who are in the market right now are serious — they've already absorbed the payment math.

2. The spread between "median" and "top of market" is wide. A $950,000 closing in a ZIP whose median runs in the low-$700Ks isn't luck — it's what condition, presentation, and pricing strategy are worth. The gap between a prepared sale and an as-is sale has rarely been bigger.

3. The Queens-to-Long-Island pipeline is alive. Queens sellers are often Long Island buyers in the same twelve months. Working both markets means your sale and your purchase can actually be coordinated instead of leaving you stranded between them.
