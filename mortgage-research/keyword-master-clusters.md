# Gadura Mortgage — Keyword Master + Intent Clusters (Queens / Nassau / Suffolk NY)

**Status:** Research-phase deliverable. Search-intent and clustering research only — no content is built or published from this document. Search volumes are not included here (no verified, dated volume-data source was pulled for this pass); before any hub is built, pull current volumes from a real keyword tool and date them, per the fail-closed rule: no unsourced number goes into a public page.
**Geography:** Queens County, Nassau County, Suffolk County, NY — same verified geographic ontology as the real estate project.
**Method:** Cluster by consumer intent into a small number of defensible hubs, not one page per keyword. Each hub below is a candidate page/section, not a commitment — final hub count is a Month 6 decision made against competitor and compliance findings.

Core intent: a buyer (often paired with a real-estate search already in progress) wants to know what they can borrow and how to get pre-approved.

- mortgage pre-approval Queens NY / Nassau County / Suffolk County
- how much mortgage can I afford Long Island / NYC
- first time home buyer mortgage Queens
- mortgage pre-qualification vs pre-approval
- documents needed for mortgage pre-approval NY
- conventional loan requirements NY
- how long does mortgage pre-approval take

**Notes:** Natural adjacency to the real estate site's existing `first-time-home-buyer-queens.html` and `buy-a-home-queens.html` — an eventual cross-link is exactly the kind of "affiliated business" touchpoint that needs the AfBA disclosure resolved first (see the Project CELL brief, §b). Do not link until that's cleared.

## Cluster 7 — Rate/Market Awareness (highest compliance sensitivity)

Core intent: "what are rates today" — highest-intent, highest-volume, and the single most compliance-sensitive cluster in this whole set.

- current mortgage rates NY / Long Island / Queens
- 30 year mortgage rate today
- mortgage rate forecast 2026

**Hard rule carried over from the Project CELL brief:** no specific rate is ever published on any page in this cluster without (1) a live, dated source per the rate-sourcing plan and (2) full Reg Z §1026.24 trigger-term disclosure review. Until the rate-sourcing plan (Month 5 deliverable) is finalized and a compliant feed exists, this cluster stays as a topic placeholder with no numeric content — general market-education copy only, sourced to a named reference (e.g., Freddie Mac PMMS) rather than a self-quoted rate.
